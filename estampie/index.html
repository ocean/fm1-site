ESTAMPIE

Coming soon.

Custom firmware for the FM-1, named after the estampie, a fast dance of the 13th and 14th centuries. A groovebox for tavern and court dances: plucked lutes and citoles, buzzing hurdy-gurdies, shawms and bagpipes over drones, frame drums, tabors and nakers, chant, and the church modes.
