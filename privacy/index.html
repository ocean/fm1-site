Privacy

The FM-SWITCH app and this website don't collect any data.

There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and nothing about you or your FM-1 is sent to me.

FM-SWITCH keeps everything on your device: the firmwares you save, and the copies Save my FM-1 makes. To show the firmware list and download firmwares, it fetches them from this site and the places they're published, the way a browser fetches a page. Tips in the app are handled by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

This site loads nothing from anywhere else. It remembers whether you chose FM-SWITCH's Standard or Retro look in your own browser, and that never leaves it. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
