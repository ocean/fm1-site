Privacy

The FM-SWITCH app doesn't collect any data, and this website only counts visits, with nothing that identifies you.

There are no accounts, advertising or tracking, and the app sends nothing about you or your FM-1 to me.

FM-SWITCH keeps everything on your device: the firmwares you save, the copies Save my FM-1 makes, and on a Mac, backups of the FM-1's flash. To show the firmware list and download firmwares, it fetches them from this site and the places they're published, the way a browser fetches a page. The Mac app also checks GitHub once a day for a newer release of FM-SWITCH, and that request carries nothing about you or your FM-1. Tips in the app are handled by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

This site counts visits to its pages with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and keeps nothing that identifies you. For each visit it notes the page, the site that linked to it, the browser, screen width and language, and the country, worked out from your network address, which it doesn't keep. Its counting script is the only thing the site loads from anywhere else. More is in GoatCounter's privacy notes.

The site remembers whether you chose FM-SWITCH's Standard or Retro look in your own browser, and that never leaves it. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
